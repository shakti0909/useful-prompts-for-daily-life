# P1: ITR Filing AI Prompts & Workflow (New Tax Regime)

> **Disclaimer:** This repository contains prompts to assist in filing **ITR-1** and **ITR-2** under the **New Tax Regime** aimed at zero or optimized tax liability. For complex tax situations, consult a certified Chartered Accountant (CA). Prompts may require adjustments as tax laws update.

---

## Overview
A structured set of prompts designed to assist individuals or future AI agents in navigating Indian Income Tax filing using AIS, TIS, and Form-16.

---

## Filing Options

### Option 1: Interactive AI Assistant Workflow
Paste the provided multi-agent [prompt](https://github.com/shakti0909/useful-prompts-for-daily-life/blob/master/itr_filing_agent_system.md) into your preferred AI assistant (Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT), followed by relevant sections of your tax documents (Form-16, AIS, TIS) as you work through each schedule on the IT portal.

#### Initial Prompt Template
```text
I will be providing a multi-agent system prompt. Use those instructions to guide me through filing my Indian Income Tax Return (ITR) under the New Tax Regime. Help me navigate my Form-16, AIS, and TIS, and walk me through filing each schedule on the official IT portal. I will paste relevant sections of my documents as needed.

My Profile Details:
[e.g., Salaried, Mutual Fund / Crypto transactions, savings interest, etc.]

System Prompt:
[PASTE MULTI-AGENT PROMPT HERE]
```

## Option 2: Automated JSON Generation
Build a multi-agent system to parse your tax documents, generate the standard ITR JSON schema, and upload it directly to the Income Tax portal. Use the base prompts provided here as foundational instructions for your agents.
